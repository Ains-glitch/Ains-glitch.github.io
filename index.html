Projects & Investigations

Research

Current work sits at the intersection of solar wind physics and space weather forecasting.

NASA OSTEM Internship · Summer 2026
Characterizing the Solar Sources and Geospace Response of Isolated Stream Interaction Regions with Embedded Periodic Mesoscale Solar Wind Structures
→ In-progress manuscript
Method

Investigating the solar sources, solar wind properties, and geospace impacts of isolated stream interaction regions (SIRs) using Wang-Sheeley-Arge (WSA) modeling and spacecraft observations, to improve understanding of ambient solar wind-driven space weather. Presenting as a poster at the 57th AAS Solar Physics Division Conference, August 2026.

Principal Investigator · Fall 2024 – Spring 2026
BoTTLE — Bounded Transient Time Leader Emissions Tracking & Strike Prediction
→ View poster
Method

Lead a NASA Florida Space Grant Consortium and ERAU-funded project predicting lightning strikes using an array of muon detectors alongside RADAR data, examining correlations between muon flux and lightning discharge. 2025 NASA FSGC Grant Winner; 2025–2026 ERAU IGNITE Grant Award Nominee.

Muon DetectionRADARAtmospheric Physics
Spring 2025
Solar Sources of Ambient Solar Wind-Driven Substorms with ADAPT-WSA
→ View poster
Method

Investigating geoeffective solar wind formation linked to substorm activity using the ADAPT-WSA model; developing methods to classify solar wind properties and identify source regions tied to geomagnetic substorms, excluding large-scale solar events.

ADAPT-WSASubstorms
Spring 2025

Photometric Variability & Habitable Zones — Castor C

Investigated stellar flare activity in the M-dwarf system Castor C using the ERAU 1-meter telescope to assess impacts on exoplanet habitability.

Spring 2025

Structural Analysis of Galaxy NGC 3665

Modeled surface brightness profiles from DECam g, r, and z filter data to infer the galaxy's evolutionary history via 1D/2D light-distribution fitting in Python.
