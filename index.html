I'm an incoming PhD student in Electrical & Computer Engineering at the University of Florida, where I'll be advised by Dr. Robert Moore.

I came to this from Embry-Riddle Aeronautical University, where I earned dual B.S. degrees in Space Physics and Astronomy & Astrophysics (Cum Laude) with a minor in Applied Mathematics. Alongside my heliophysics research, I led a NASA Florida Space Grant funded project using muon detection to analyze thunderstorm muon propagation, and attended two summer schools at HAARP and NASA's SHIELD DRIVE Center.

This summer, I'm working as a NASA Office of STEM Engagement (OSTEM) intern, investigating the solar sources and geospace response of isolated stream interaction regions.

Outside of research, I enjoy observational astronomy, working with the ERAU 1-meter Cassegrain telescope, and exploring connections between space weather and everyday technology.

Projects & Investigations

Research

Current work sits at the intersection of solar wind physics and space weather forecasting.

NASA OSTEM Internship · Summer 2026
Characterizing the Solar Sources and Geospace Response of Isolated Stream Interaction Regions with Embedded Periodic Mesoscale Solar Wind Structures
→ In progress manuscript
Method

Investigating the solar sources, solar wind properties, and geospace impacts of isolated stream interaction regions (SIRs) using Wang-Sheeley-Arge (WSA) modeling and spacecraft observations, to improve understanding of ambient solar wind driven space weather. Presenting as a poster at the 57th AAS Solar Physics Division Conference, August 2026.

WSA ModelSIRsSpace Weather
Principal Investigator · Fall 2024 – Spring 2026
BoTTLE: Bounded Thunderstorm Tracking of Lightning Events with muon detection
→ View poster
Method

Led a NASA Florida Space Grant Consortium and ERAU-funded project utilizing muon detectors to systematically monitor muon particle activity in conjunction with observed lightning events. Our objective is to examine whether significant correlations exist between decreasing muon flux and the occurrence of lightning discharges. 2025 NASA FSGC Grant Winner; 2025-2026 ERAU IGNITE Grant Award Nominee.

Muon DetectionAtmospheric Physics
Spring 2025
Solar Sources of Ambient Solar Wind Driven Substorms with ADAPT-WSA
→ View poster
Method

Investigating geoeffective solar wind formation linked to substorm activity using the ADAPT-WSA model; developing methods to classify solar wind properties and identify source regions tied to geomagnetic substorms, excluding large-scale solar events.

ADAPT-WSASubstorms
Spring 2025

Photometric Variability & Habitable Zones: Castor C

Investigated stellar flare activity in the M-dwarf system Castor C using the ERAU 1-meter telescope to assess impacts on exoplanet habitability.

Spring 2025

Structural Analysis of Galaxy NGC 3665

Modeled surface brightness profiles from DECam g, r, and z filter data to infer the galaxy's evolutionary history via 1D/2D light distribution fitting in Python.
